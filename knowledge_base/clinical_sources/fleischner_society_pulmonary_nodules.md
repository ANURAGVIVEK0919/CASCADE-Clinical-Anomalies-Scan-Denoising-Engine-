# Fleischner Society Guidelines for Management of Incidental Pulmonary Nodules

**Source Reference**: MacMahon H, Naidich DP, Goo JM, et al. Guidelines for Management of Incidental Pulmonary Nodules Detected on CT Images: From the Fleischner Society 2017. *Radiology*. 2017;284(1):228-243. doi:10.1148/radiol.2017161659
**License / Terms**: Published clinical consensus summary for educational and algorithmic reference.
**Domain**: Thoracic Computed Tomography (Chest CT)

---

## 1. Scope & Patient Population
These guidelines apply to incidental solid and subsolid pulmonary nodules detected on CT scans in patients 35 years of age or older. They do not apply to lung cancer screening, patients with known primary malignancies, or immunocompromised patients.

## 2. Solid Nodules Management

### Single Solid Nodules
* **Nodules < 6 mm (< 100 mm³)**:
  * Low risk: No routine follow-up CT required.
  * High risk: Optional follow-up CT at 12 months in selected high-risk patients.
* **Nodules 6 to 8 mm (100–250 mm³)**:
  * Low risk: Follow-up CT at 6–12 months; consider repeat CT at 18–24 months if suspicious morphology.
  * High risk: Follow-up CT at 6–12 months, followed by repeat CT at 18–24 months.
* **Nodules > 8 mm (> 250 mm³)**:
  * Low or high risk: Consider follow-up CT at 3 months, PET-CT, or tissue biopsy depending on pretest probability of malignancy.

### Multiple Solid Nodules
* **Nodules < 6 mm**: No routine follow-up for low risk; optional CT at 12 months for high risk.
* **Nodules >= 6 mm**: Follow-up CT at 3–6 months, then repeat at 18–24 months based on most suspicious nodule.

---

## 3. Subsolid Nodules Management

### Pure Ground-Glass Nodules (GGN)
* **< 6 mm**: No routine follow-up recommended.
* **>= 6 mm**: Follow-up CT at 6–12 months to confirm persistence, then follow-up every 2 years until 5 years.

### Part-Solid Nodules
* **< 6 mm**: No routine follow-up recommended.
* **>= 6 mm**: Follow-up CT at 3–6 months to confirm persistence. If solid component >= 6 mm, consider PET-CT, biopsy, or resection.
