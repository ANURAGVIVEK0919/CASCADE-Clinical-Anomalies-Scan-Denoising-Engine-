## Section 1: Target Population and Diagnostic Criteria
LI-RADS applies to patients at high risk for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC):
- Adults with cirrhosis from any etiology (excluding cirrhosis from congenital hepatic fibrosis or vascular disorders).
- Adult chronic hepatitis B carriers regardless of cirrhosis status.
- Current or prior HCC patients.

LI-RADS does NOT apply to:
- Patients younger than 18 years.
- Cirrhosis due to congenital hepatic fibrosis.
- Cirrhosis due to vascular disorders (Budd-Chiari syndrome, hereditary hemorrhagic telangiectasia, cardiac cirrhosis).

## Section 2: Major Imaging Features for HCC
On multiphase contrast-enhanced CT or MRI, major imaging features determine categorization (LR-1 through LR-5):
1. **Nonrim Arterial Phase Hyperenhancement (APHE)**:
   - Enhancement in whole or in part of observation unequivocally greater in degree than surrounding liver parenchyma during late arterial phase.
2. **Observation Size**:
   - Longest outer-edge-to-outer-edge dimension.
3. **Nonperipheral "Washout" Appearance**:
   - Visually assessed temporal reduction in enhancement relative to composite liver parenchyma from earlier to later phases.
4. **Enhancing Capsule**:
   - Smooth, uniform, sharp border around all or most of an observation, unequivocally thicker or more conspicuous than surrounding parenchyma.
5. **Threshold Growth**:
   - Size increase of >= 50% in <= 6 months.

## Section 3: Diagnostic Categories
- **LR-1**: Definitely Benign (simple cyst, typical hemangioma).
- **LR-2**: Probably Benign (probable hemangioma, focal nodular hyperplasia-like lesion).
- **LR-3**: Intermediate Probability of Malignancy (needs multidisciplinary follow-up).
- **LR-4**: Probably HCC (features suggestive of HCC but not meeting definitive LR-5 criteria).
- **LR-5**: Definitely HCC (100% specificity required; candidates for transplantation/ablation without mandatory biopsy).
- **LR-M**: Probably or definitely malignant, not specific for HCC (e.g., intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma).
